# Cardcode

**Short Description (Max 80 chars):**
Generate beautiful, offline-first QR cards for Wi-Fi, links, and contacts.

**Full Description:**
Cardcode is the minimalist "Print Shop" in your pocket for generating crisp, stylish QR codes. Perfect for cafes, Airbnbs, or your own home, Cardcode lets you easily create beautifully designed, printable QR cards to share your Wi-Fi, direct people to a link, or seamlessly exchange contact details. 

Say goodbye to generic, robotic-looking QR codes. Choose from four premium themes—Ink, Paper, Forest, and Midnight—and let our smart engine automatically layout your card with readable typography and soft shapes. Once generated, instantly share the card as a high-resolution PNG, ready to print.

Features:
- **Three Core Templates:** Quick setups for Wi-Fi (WPA/WEP/None), URL Links, and Contact Details.
- **Four Premium Themes:** Carefully curated color palettes and high-contrast designs for reliable scanning.
- **Offline First:** Zero permissions required. No tracking, no database, no internet needed. 
- **High-Resolution Export:** Cards are rendered as crystal-clear 1080 x 1528 PNGs—ideal for A6 printing.
- **Accessible & Adaptive:** Full support for TalkBack, dynamic font scaling, and modern Android adaptive icons.
